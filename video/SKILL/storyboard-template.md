# BLINK launch video — storyboard

30.0 s · 1920 × 1080 · 60 fps · 1800 frames

For every scene write four things: what is on screen, how it moves, where the camera goes, and how long it stays. Scene lengths must add up to the total.

## Example (filled)

**Scene 1 · Paper pile-up (hook)** — 0:00–0:04, frames 0–240
- On screen: dark background. Handwritten sales notes ("Anote venta", "Cuenta pendiente", "Recibo #42", "Cuadre de caja", "Inventario a mano"…) drop in and stack into a pile. Counter top-right: "Manual notes 0 → 150". Kinetic headline: "Still recording sales by hand?" Blink mascot in the corner looking tired.
- Motion: cards drop with overshoot, staggered. Counter counts up over ~1 s. Headline word by word.
- Camera: slow push-in 100% → 106%.

**Scene 2 · Manual grind (problem)** — 0:04–0:11, frames 240–660
- On screen: three cluttered windows: old notebook scan, spreadsheet, manual calculator. Cursor types numbers in the calculator, arcs over and pastes into the spreadsheet, hesitates, fixes an error. Text: "Slow. Manual. Exhausting."
- Motion: cursor on arcs, click presses, highlighted rows. Blink mascot shakes head gently.
- Camera: easy-ease pan left → right, following the cursor.

**Scene 3 · BLINK kicks in (fix)** — 0:11–0:19, frames 660–1140
- On screen: BLINK Liquid Glass dashboard (Figma SVG) shown seamlessly across PC and mobile browser without downloads. Cursor clicks the central "$" Sell button. Four Bento Grid cards come in: Total Sales, Paid Orders, Average Ticket, Gross Margin. Sales and metrics update in real time.
- Motion: Sell button press (scale 1 → 0.96 → 1). Cards staggered, each card animates with smooth counters and bar charts. Blink mascot nods with confident approval.
- Camera: zoom into the bottom/main action bar on the click (≈130%), then ease back to 100% for the cards.

**Scene 4 · Payoff** — 0:19–0:25, frames 1140–1500
- On screen: green checkmarks (`#34c759`) appear on the financial cards. Manual work counter drops 150 → 0. Text: "Zero paperwork. Pure speed."
- Motion: counter counts down over ~1 s. Hold the clear Liquid Glass dashboard ≥ 1.5 s. Blink mascot smiles cheerfully.
- Camera: pull back to reveal the whole dashboard across PC and phone, clean and organized.

**Scene 5 · Logo + CTA** — 0:25–0:30, frames 1500–1800
- On screen: dashboard scales away. BLINK logo builds. Tagline: "Vende en un parpadeo, factura con BLINK ;)". CTA: "Empieza gratis · blink POS".
- Motion: logo build, tagline with offset, slow drift to the end. Blink mascot gives a final wink.
- Camera: gentle push-in.
