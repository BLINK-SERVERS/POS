# BLINK — 30-second launch video

**Company:** BLINK is a cloud-based smart point-of-sale (POS) platform that takes over slow manual bookkeeping: recording sales in a flash, syncing inventory, and building real-time financial reports from any phone or PC without downloading anything.

**Audience:** store owners, entrepreneurs, and retail managers at SMBs.

**Goal:** launch video for Product Hunt, the website hero, and paid social.

**One message:** Vende en un parpadeo, factura con BLINK ;)

**Must show:**
1. The hook: a merchant buried in handwritten sales notes and slow calculations.
2. The problem: wasting hours registering sales by hand and fixing manual mistakes across notebooks and spreadsheets.
3. The product: fast checkout and real-time Bento Grid metrics in our real Liquid Glass UI across PC and mobile.
4. The payoff: zero paperwork, hours saved, and clear financial reports in one tap.
5. Logo + CTA.

**Tone:** confident, clean, fast. Premium SaaS launch, not cartoonish. Synced with voiceover, music + SFX, and subtle reactions from the Blink mascot.

**Deliverables:** 30 s master in 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 60 fps), plus 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns.

**Assets:** Apple UI tool Kit, React Files web, sketchfiles, (Liquid Glass light/dark mode), brand guide (colors `#0088ff`, `#34c759`, `#FFFEFB`, font SF Pro), Blink mascot SVG, logo SVG, background images.

**Deadline:** before launch.
